\subsection{Construction of derived variables}

This should contain the operational rules for variables that are not simply raw survey answers.

Most importantly, spell out \(C_{ig}\):

$$ C_{ig}= \frac{C_{ig}^{\mathrm{Work}}+C_{ig}^{\mathrm{NonWork}}}{2} $$

when both measures are available, whereas

$$ C_{ig}=C_{ig}^{\mathrm{NonWork}} $$

when workplace contact is genuinely not applicable. That is already the rule in your main text.

Also include the construction of

$$ Positive_{ig}=\max(Q_{ig}-4,0) $$

and

$$ Negative_{ig}=\max(4-Q_{ig},0), $$

and any other transformations/codings that are not immediately obvious from the raw questionnaire.
